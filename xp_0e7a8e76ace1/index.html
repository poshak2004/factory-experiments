Monthly books for your Pune housing society, done for you over WhatsApp

For treasurers, secretaries and chairmen of co-operative housing societies with 20 to 60 flats and no paid manager.

The problem

In many societies a volunteer does the monthly accounts: matching bank credits to flats, issuing maintenance receipts, tracking dues and defaulters, and keeping vouchers and the bank reconciliation ready for the annual audit. Accounting software exists, but someone still has to run it.

What we plan to do

You forward the bank statement and bills on WhatsApp. We prepare:

  • Maintenance receipts
  • The dues and defaulter ledger
  • The monthly bank reconciliation
  • An audit-ready voucher file

What we do not do

  • We do not do the statutory audit. Your society's own auditor still does that.
  • We do not do any work reserved for chartered accountants.
  • The service is not live yet. Joining the waitlist does not start any service.
