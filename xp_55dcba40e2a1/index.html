A real person calls your lapsed patients and books them into your diary

For owner-dentists of 1–3 chair clinics in Pune who have patients who never came back to finish treatment.

Who this is for

Owner-dentists of independent Pune clinics with 1–3 chairs and little or no front-desk staff, who decide on spend themselves and can name at least 10 patients from the last 6 months who lapsed or left a treatment plan unfinished (for example, a root canal without the crown, or a missed follow-up).

How the planned service works

  1. You share a short list: patient first name, phone number and the pending procedure. Nothing else.
  2. A person calls them in Hindi, Marathi or English, on behalf of your clinic.
  3. Patients who agree are booked into your diary. You keep your patients and your records.
  4. You get a simple report of who was reached.

Right now we are only checking whether clinic owners want this. We are not taking any patient data on this page.

Planned price (a hypothesis)

We are thinking of ₹2,500 per month. Terms such as any refund are not decided. A pay-per-completed-visit option (about ₹500–800) is also being considered. These are ideas we want your reaction to, not final terms. No money is taken on this page.

Honest limits

  • The service has not launched. We have not yet shown that calls bring patients back.
  • We cannot promise that any patient will book or attend. Cost or fear of treatment may keep some patients away, and a call may not change that.
  • Your own staff calling the same list is a free alternative, and some clinic software already sends automated reminders. A human call may or may not do better; that is part of what we want to learn.
  • Patient data is not collected at this stage.
