The problem

If a supplier has not filed GSTR-1 (or filed wrong details), their invoice does not show in your GSTR-2B. Under Section 16(2)(aa) of the CGST Act, input tax credit (ITC) on that invoice is blocked until it appears. Under Section 16(4) there is a deadline, so credit can be lost permanently if the supplier files too late. The usual advice is to follow up with the supplier.

Many small businesses are busy and follow-up gets postponed. Please check dates and rules with your own CA; this page is not tax advice.

What we plan to offer in the pilot

  • You give us a list of named suppliers whose invoices are missing from your GSTR-2B, plus your written authorisation to contact them on your behalf.
  • A person (not software) follows up with those suppliers, in English, Hindi or Marathi.
  • You receive a report of which credit appeared in GSTR-2B.

Limits of the service

  • We cannot make a supplier file. Some suppliers may not respond, and it is possible that no credit appears.
  • We do not know yet how suppliers react to a call from a third party. That is untested.
  • We do not give tax or legal advice.

Who it is for

Owners or accountants of regular-GST traders and distributors in Pune with many purchase suppliers, who have ITC missing from GSTR-2B for 60+ days and have not been able to fix it through payment holds or their CA.
