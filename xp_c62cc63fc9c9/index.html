What this is

We are an early-stage team testing whether we can help independent PG owners in Hinjewadi, Wakad, Marunji and Maan (Pune) fill vacant beds. This service is in development and is not live. This page is a waitlist for a small early-access pilot.

हम परख रहे हैं कि क्या हम खाली बेड भरने में मदद कर सकते हैं। यह सेवा अभी शुरू नहीं हुई है; यह अर्ली-एक्सेस वेटलिस्ट है।

The offer we want to test

  • You would pay only if a bed is actually filled.
  • The fee we have in mind is about 50% of one month's rent for that bed. This is indicative only, not final.
  • Joining the waitlist costs nothing. We take no payment and ask for none.
  • We cannot say whether any tenants will be found or any beds filled.

फीस अभी केवल अनुमान है (लगभग एक महीने के किराये का 50%), अंतिम नहीं। कोई भुगतान नहीं लिया जाता। किरायेदारों की कोई गारंटी नहीं।

Who it is for

Owners or decision-makers of independent PGs or hostels with about 10 to 40 beds in the four localities above. We are starting with a 1-bed trial for a few owners. Branded operators are not the target.
